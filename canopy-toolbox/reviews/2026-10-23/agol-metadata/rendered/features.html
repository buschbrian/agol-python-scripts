Millcreek buildings and canopy cover zones (2023 lidar), planning estimate

Building footprints with lidar roof heights, and tree canopy cover by ownership zone, for Millcreek, Utah, from 2023 lidar. Unvalidated planning estimate; not surveyed or field-verified.

Planning estimate. This layer is an unvalidated estimate derived from lidar by City of Millcreek GIS. It has not been surveyed, engineered or checked in the field. Use it for planning, screening and display, not for legal, engineering, property-boundary, permitting or code-compliance decisions.

What it shows

Two layers for Millcreek, Utah:

  • Buildings (22,042 polygons): Salt Lake County's building footprints inside or crossing the city limits, each with roof-height statistics measured from the 2023 lidar. 21,562 footprints have a height; 480 have no lidar roof inside them and their height fields are empty (possible reasons include buildings demolished since the County drew them, very small structures and roofs hidden under trees).
  • Canopy cover zones (13 polygons): the share of the ground covered by tree canopy at least 2 m tall, for the whole city and for groups of land by ownership (private, public and its owner groups, uncertain owner, and land outside every parcel). Citywide canopy cover is 25.59% of the observed area (2,103 acres).

Typical uses: a citywide look at building heights and massing, finding tall or unusual buildings for review, canopy goals and comparisons between ownership groups. Heights are roof minus ground, not building heights as a building code defines them.

How it was made

  • Roofs. Every lidar point was classified three independent ways: this project's own first classification, the IGN FRACTAL model, and Esri's pretrained building model. A point counts as roof when at least two of the three call it building. The roof surface is the highest first or single return in each 0.5 m cell from those points.
  • Ground. The bare-earth terrain is interpolated at 0.5 m from the ground-classified returns (under a building it is interpolated from the ground around it).
  • Heights. For every 0.5 m cell whose centre lies inside a footprint (and inside the city), roof elevation minus terrain elevation. A footprint's median (HEIGHT_P50_M), 95th percentile and maximum are taken over the cells that have both a roof and terrain value. Footprint shapes and County attributes are unchanged.
  • Canopy. A canopy height model at 0.5 m: vegetation returns minus terrain, with roofs masked out. Canopy is any observed cell at least 2 m above the ground. Cover for a zone is canopy cells as a share of the zone's observed cells, counted by cell centres.
  • Zones. From the City's parcel layer: parcels grouped by owner with fixed rules (named public agencies, schools, housing authorities and districts are public; everyone else, including utilities, churches and nonprofits, is private; missing or mixed owners are uncertain), dissolved by group and clipped to the city. "No parcel" is the city outside every parcel, mostly streets and other rights-of-way. Zones overlap: the whole-city zone contains all the others and the public owner groups sit inside "Public / government parcels", so do not add areas across rows.

Accuracy and checks

  • Roof classification, against 1,217 hand-labelled roof and non-roof parts on one 1 km tile (a deliberately hard sample, not a citywide accuracy): 92.8% of roofs found; 2.8% of non-roof parts called roof.
  • Vegetation classification, same tile: 95.9% of tree parts called vegetation; 10.7% of non-vegetation parts called vegetation; shrubs 76.5%.
  • Roof support. 2,380 footprints have a lidar roof on less than 80% of their area (LOW_ROOF_SUPPORT). Their heights are less reliable: lower roofs, porches and carports are often the part missed. Of the footprints with at least 95% support, the 3D blocks built from these heights are within 1 m of the median roof for all but 13 of 14,229.
  • Canopy coverage. About 99.4% of every zone is observed by the lidar; the low and high bounds in the layer show how far the unobserved cells could move each figure (about half a point at most).
  • Copies. Every value and every footprint shape was checked against the source library before upload (0 rows differ), and the projection to Web Mercator was checked vertex by vertex (all within 0.07 mm of Esri's own projection with the ITRF08 transformation).
  • None of these figures is a field validation. No building height has been compared with a survey or a plan set.

Limits

  • Heights are roof-minus-terrain estimates, not code-defined building heights (which are measured to a defined point of the roof from a defined grade). Chimneys, rooftop equipment, parapets and branches over a roof raise the maximum; the median is the safest single figure.
  • Footprints are the County's. A footprint can hold several buildings, a building can have several footprints, and buildings built or demolished since the County drew them show up as missing or empty roofs. 22,042 is a count of footprint records, not of buildings.
  • Many County fields are empty in this snapshot (BLDGHEIGHT, SOURCE, most ADDRESS and BuiltYear values). They are carried unchanged so the layer joins back to the County's.
  • Canopy at 2 m includes tall shrubs and excludes lower plants. Leaf-off trees in early November may be under-counted where few returns came back from bare branches.
  • Do not use Shape__Area or Shape__Length for measurement. ArcGIS Online computes them in Web Mercator, which inflates areas here by about 1.74 times and lengths by about 1.32 times. Use FOOTPRINT_M2 and ZONE_ACRES, which were computed in UTM before upload.

Coordinates and heights. Built in NAD83(2011) / UTM zone 12N (EPSG 6341), metres, and taken to WGS84 / Web Mercator for ArcGIS Online with the transformation WGS_1984_(ITRF08)_To_NAD_1983_2011. Every layer in the Millcreek LiDAR 2023 set uses this same transformation, so the layers line up with each other and with Esri's basemaps to a few centimetres; without it they would sit about 1.2 m apart. Elevations are NAVD88 (Geoid18) in metres; heights are metres above the lidar bare-earth terrain.

Lidar source. The 2023 Salt Lake Valley QL1 lidar (USGS project UT_2023SaltLakeCo_C24, USGS 3D Elevation Program), flown on October 7, November 2 and November 4, 2023. Quality level 1 means at least 8 pulses per square metre and a vertical accuracy (RMSEz) of 10 cm or better on open, non-vegetated ground under the USGS specification; the vendor's own accuracy report for this project has not been reviewed for these products. The points were reclassified for these products (see "How it was made"); the products are gridded at 0.5 m.

HEIGHT_STATUS flags

Every footprint carries UNVALIDATED; the other flags are added, separated by semicolons, when they apply (for example UNVALIDATED;LOW_ROOF_SUPPORT).

Flag Footprints Meaning
UNVALIDATED 22,042 Every record: no height has been checked against a survey.
LOW_ROOF_SUPPORT 2,380 Lidar roof on less than 80% of the footprint's cells (ROOF_COV_PCT under 80). A review flag, not an accuracy threshold.
NO_ROOF_SUPPORT 477 No cell in the footprint has a lidar roof; height fields are empty. Often a demolished, very small or tree-covered building.
NEGATIVE_HEIGHT 35 At least one cell has its roof below the terrain (NEG_H_CELLS above 0). Those cells are kept; no footprint has a negative median.
PARTIAL_AOI 27 The footprint crosses the city limit; statistics use only the part inside (AOI_PCT under 100).
GEOMETRY_REPAIRED 5 The County polygon was invalid and was repaired (area change under 0.000000001 m2).
NO_CELL_CENTERS 3 A sliver inside the city that holds no 0.5 m cell centre; no statistics.

The Millcreek LiDAR 2023 set. Eight layers from the same lidar and the same processing: buildings and canopy cover zones (feature layer), 3D buildings (scene layer), bare-earth terrain (elevation layer), hillshade (tile layer), slope classes, 2 m contours, treetops and tree crowns (vector tile layers). They share one coordinate transformation and one terrain, so they can be stacked in one map or scene.

Questions or corrections: City of Millcreek GIS. The processing records (scripts, checks and run logs) are kept by City of Millcreek GIS.

Terms of use

These data are unvalidated planning estimates produced by City of Millcreek GIS from 2023 lidar. They are not surveyed, engineered or field-verified, and must not be used to determine property boundaries or ownership, building or site design, engineering, flood or drainage design, code compliance (including building height), or any legal or regulatory determination. They are provided as is, without warranty of accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose; check conditions on the ground before relying on them. Credit "City of Millcreek GIS; 2023 Salt Lake Valley QL1 lidar (USGS 3DEP)" when you use or show them.

Salt Lake County data. The building footprints and their County attributes come from Salt Lake County (Assessor and Surveyor). The County's own terms apply to them: the County states that its footprints are not field verified, are for general reference only, and are not intended to determine property ownership, final building and site design or jurisdictional boundaries, or to replace a certified topographical or boundary survey or any other legal document.

Parcels. Ownership groups come from the City of Millcreek parcel layer (2025 tax-year snapshot, read October 8, 2026). A tax year is not an ownership effective date, and the grouping of owners into private, public and uncertain is the City's own rule set for this analysis, not a legal finding of ownership.
