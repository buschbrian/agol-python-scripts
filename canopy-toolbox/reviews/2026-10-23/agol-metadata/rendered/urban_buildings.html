Millcreek 3D buildings for ArcGIS Urban, UTM 12N (2023 lidar), planning estimate

21,561 Millcreek, Utah, buildings in 3D, with roof forms from the 2023 lidar and an estimated floor count, in UTM 12N for a local ArcGIS Urban model. Unvalidated planning estimate; not surveyed or field-verified.

Planning estimate. This layer is an unvalidated estimate derived from lidar by City of Millcreek GIS. It has not been surveyed, engineered or checked in the field. Use it for planning, screening and display, not for legal, engineering, property-boundary, permitting or code-compliance decisions.

What it shows

The same 21,561 Millcreek, Utah, buildings as the public Millcreek 3D buildings with roof forms layer, in NAD83(2011) UTM zone 12N instead of WGS84, for the existing buildings of a local ArcGIS Urban model in that coordinate system. For 20,730 the roof has a modelled shape from the 2023 lidar (roofer LoD 2.2); 647 use Esri's LOD2.0 roof forms and 184 are flat LOD1.2 blocks. The LEVEL field says which.

Floors for ArcGIS Urban

FLOORS is an estimated floor count for Urban's Convert existing buildings (attribute-driven mode): the 95th percentile lidar roof height above ground (HEIGHT_P95_M) divided by 3.5 m, rounded to the nearest whole number (halves up), and never less than 1. No building lacks a height, so none is empty. It is a planning estimate from roof height, not a count of storeys: a tall single-storey building, a pitched roof or a split-level house can be given the wrong number.

Fields

Every building carries its County footprint attributes, the lidar roof-height statistics and the model it uses. Unlike the public layer, this copy does not carry the roof-plane attributes (areas, slopes, azimuth) added on October 24; use the public layer for those. Heights and elevations are metres; Z50 fields are elevations, other height fields are above local ground.

How it was made

  1. The three-way hybrid buildings of October 25, 2026 (see the public layer for how roofer and the Esri fallbacks were chosen).
  2. Field types a scene layer package cannot carry were to be dropped first (big integer, date only, time only, timestamp offset); the source has none. FLOORS was added.
  3. Packaged as a scene layer package in UTM 12N (EPSG 6341) with NAVD88 height (EPSG 5703), no transformation, and published on October 26, 2026. A 20-building test package was built and checked first.

Accuracy and checks

  • The package and the hosted layer hold 21,561 buildings; their FP_ID count and sum (21,561 and 237,493,394) equal the source's, and the model counts per LEVEL match. FLOORS on the hosted layer sums to 32,836, from 1 to 12.
  • ArcGIS Pro's package validation reports one warning: "vertex normals may be required by Esri products". Pro writes no vertex normals for a package in a projected coordinate system (the same source packaged in WGS84 has them and no warning); viewers then compute shading themselves.
  • On the cells where the lidar saw roof, the buildings are within 0.5 m of the lidar roof on 96.7% of cells and within 1 m on 98.1%. The lidar is also what the roofs were built from, so this is fidelity to the lidar, not independent accuracy.
  • Seen drawing in the local ArcGIS Urban model on October 27, 2026, on the UTM basemap and terrain (seen from above only).

Limits

  • Planning estimates, not surveyed buildings. Heights are roof minus ground from lidar, not code-defined building heights.
  • Footprints are the County's, as of October 19, 2026; buildings built or removed since are not reflected.
  • Complex roofs can come out fragmented; under trees, glass and dark roofs the lidar sees less.

Coordinates and heights. NAD83(2011) / UTM zone 12N (EPSG 6341), metres: the lidar's own coordinate system, with no datum transformation. This copy is for a local scene or a local ArcGIS Urban model in 6341. The other layers in the Millcreek LiDAR 2023 set are WGS84 / Web Mercator copies for global scenes; they hold the same data. Elevations are NAVD88 (Geoid18) in metres; heights are metres above the lidar bare-earth terrain.

Lidar source. The 2023 Salt Lake Valley QL1 lidar (USGS project UT_2023SaltLakeCo_C24, USGS 3D Elevation Program), flown on October 7, November 2 and November 4, 2023. Quality level 1 means at least 8 pulses per square metre and a vertical accuracy (RMSEz) of 10 cm or better on open, non-vegetated ground under the USGS specification; the vendor's own accuracy report for this project has not been reviewed for these products. For these products the building and vegetation classes of the points were refined; the ground class was not changed. All products are gridded at 0.5 m.

The Millcreek ArcGIS Urban layers. Organization-only layers for ArcGIS Urban. For a local model in UTM 12N: 3D buildings (scene layer), building footprints (feature layer), bare-earth terrain (elevation layer) and a light basemap on the terrain's tiling scheme. For a global (Web Mercator) model: the same 3D buildings in WGS84. They come from the same lidar, the same buildings and the same terrain as the public Millcreek LiDAR 2023 set.

Questions or corrections: City of Millcreek GIS. The processing records (scripts, checks and run logs) are kept by City of Millcreek GIS.
