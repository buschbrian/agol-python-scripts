Millcreek building footprints for ArcGIS Urban, UTM 12N (2023 lidar), planning estimate

Footprints of the 21,561 Millcreek, Utah, 3D buildings with lidar roof height, model level and an estimated floor count, in UTM 12N for a local ArcGIS Urban model. Unvalidated planning estimate; not surveyed or field-verified.

Planning estimate. This layer is an unvalidated estimate derived from lidar by City of Millcreek GIS. It has not been surveyed, engineered or checked in the field. Use it for planning, screening and display, not for legal, engineering, property-boundary, permitting or code-compliance decisions.

What it shows

One polygon for each of the 21,561 buildings in the scene layer Millcreek 3D buildings for ArcGIS Urban, UTM 12N, with the same FP_ID, so the two can be joined or used together as the existing buildings of a local ArcGIS Urban model. Each footprint is the outline of its 3D model seen from above. It carries the roof height, the model level and an estimated floor count, FLOORS, for Urban's Convert existing buildings in attribute-driven mode. There are no owner or address fields.

Floors

FLOORS is the 95th percentile lidar roof height above ground (HEIGHT_P95_M) divided by 3.5 m, rounded to the nearest whole number (halves up), and never less than 1; it would be empty for a building with no height, and there are none. It is a planning estimate from roof height, not a count of storeys. 11,894 buildings get 1, 8,271 get 2, 1,294 get 3, 101 get 4 to 9 and one gets 12; the sum is 32,836.

How it was made

  1. The 21,561 buildings of the October 25, 2026 three-way hybrid (roofer LoD 2.2 roofs, Esri LOD2.0 and LOD1.2 fallbacks).
  2. ArcGIS Pro's Multipatch Footprint, grouped by FP_ID: one polygon per building, none empty.
  3. HEIGHT_P95_M and LEVEL copied from the building; FLOORS computed; stored in UTM 12N and published on October 26, 2026.

Accuracy and checks

  • The hosted layer holds 21,561 footprints. FP_ID count and sum (21,561 and 237,493,394) equal the scene layer's and the source's; FP_IDs are unique. LEVEL counts are 20,730 roofer LoD2.2, 647 Esri LOD2.0 and 184 Esri LOD1.2. No FLOORS value is empty.
  • Shape__Area totals 4,710,583 m2 on the hosted layer, within 0.001 m2 of the area computed in UTM before upload. In UTM, Shape__Area and Shape__Length are grid measures: the UTM scale factor is about 0.9997 at Millcreek, so areas read about 0.07% under the ground area and lengths about 0.03% under.
  • The outlines follow the 3D models, which follow the County footprints; they are not redrawn from the lidar.

Limits

  • Planning estimates. Heights are roof minus ground from lidar, not code-defined building heights; FLOORS is an estimate.
  • Footprints are the County's, as of October 19, 2026; buildings built or removed since are not reflected.
  • The field LEVEL is named LEVEL_ here: ArcGIS Online renames LEVEL, a reserved word, when it publishes. The scene layer keeps LEVEL.

Coordinates and heights. NAD83(2011) / UTM zone 12N (EPSG 6341), metres: the lidar's own coordinate system, with no datum transformation. This copy is for a local scene or a local ArcGIS Urban model in 6341. The other layers in the Millcreek LiDAR 2023 set are WGS84 / Web Mercator copies for global scenes; they hold the same data. Elevations are NAVD88 (Geoid18) in metres; heights are metres above the lidar bare-earth terrain.

Lidar source. The 2023 Salt Lake Valley QL1 lidar (USGS project UT_2023SaltLakeCo_C24, USGS 3D Elevation Program), flown on October 7, November 2 and November 4, 2023. Quality level 1 means at least 8 pulses per square metre and a vertical accuracy (RMSEz) of 10 cm or better on open, non-vegetated ground under the USGS specification; the vendor's own accuracy report for this project has not been reviewed for these products. For these products the building and vegetation classes of the points were refined; the ground class was not changed. All products are gridded at 0.5 m.

The Millcreek ArcGIS Urban layers. Organization-only layers for ArcGIS Urban. For a local model in UTM 12N: 3D buildings (scene layer), building footprints (feature layer), bare-earth terrain (elevation layer) and a light basemap on the terrain's tiling scheme. For a global (Web Mercator) model: the same 3D buildings in WGS84. They come from the same lidar, the same buildings and the same terrain as the public Millcreek LiDAR 2023 set.

Questions or corrections: City of Millcreek GIS. The processing records (scripts, checks and run logs) are kept by City of Millcreek GIS.
