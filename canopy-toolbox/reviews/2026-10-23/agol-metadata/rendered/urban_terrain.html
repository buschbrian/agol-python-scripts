Millcreek bare-earth terrain for ArcGIS Urban, UTM 12N 0.5 m (2023 lidar), planning estimate

Bare-earth ground elevation for Millcreek, Utah, at 0.5 m from the 2023 lidar, metres NAVD88, as the elevation layer of a local ArcGIS Urban model in UTM 12N. Unvalidated planning estimate; not surveyed or field-verified.

Planning estimate. This layer is an unvalidated estimate derived from lidar by City of Millcreek GIS. It has not been surveyed, engineered or checked in the field. Use it for planning, screening and display, not for legal, engineering, property-boundary, permitting or code-compliance decisions.

What it shows

The elevation of the bare ground (buildings and vegetation removed) across Millcreek, Utah, and the surrounding lidar coverage, at 0.5 m, as an elevation layer in NAD83(2011) UTM zone 12N. It is the ground for a local ArcGIS Urban model or a local scene in that coordinate system, where Esri's World Elevation cannot be used. The public Millcreek bare-earth terrain 0.5 m layer holds the same ground in Web Mercator for global scenes.

Using it in ArcGIS Urban

  • A local Urban model has no default ground, so this layer is its whole ground. Outside the lidar coverage there is no elevation.
  • Urban requires the elevation layer and the basemap to share a coordinate system and a tiling scheme. The model's basemap must therefore be a tiled layer in UTM 12N on this layer's tiling scheme (origin, tile size and levels below). The tiling scheme file is kept with the processing records; a basemap built on it may add finer levels (0.25 m and below).
  • The 3D buildings for ArcGIS Urban were built on this terrain and use the same coordinates and heights.

How it was made

  1. The lidar's ground-classified returns (class 2) were interpolated to a 0.5 m bare-earth surface. Under buildings and dense vegetation the ground is interpolated from the nearest ground returns. The unmasked mosaic is used: on every city cell it is identical to the city terrain, and it carries the ground on past the city boundary.
  2. A tiling scheme was generated in UTM 12N whose finest level is the source's 0.5 m, so each finest-level pixel sits on a source cell corner and no level is coarser than the data needs. The surface was cached on it as LERC elevation tiles (0.01 m) and published as a hosted elevation layer on October 26, 2026.

Accuracy and checks

  • Compression and tiling. 300 points (150 flat, 150 steep) at their nearest 0.5 m pixel against the city terrain interpolated at the same place: median difference 0.005 m, maximum 0.010 m.
  • Against the source cell a clicked point falls in, differences reach 0.68 m on steep ground (median 0.16 m) and 0.025 m on flat ground: the pixel sits on a cell corner, not the cell centre.
  • The hosted service is an elevation layer in 6341 with the same origin and levels as the package; tiles fetched from it are byte for byte the package's.
  • The ground itself has not been checked against field survey. QL1 lidar is specified to 10 cm vertically (RMSEz) on open, non-vegetated ground; this project's vendor accuracy report has not been reviewed.
  • Not yet seen in a local scene or in ArcGIS Urban, which needs a basemap on the same tiling scheme first.

Limits

  • Ground as it was in October and November 2023; grading since then is not reflected.
  • Under buildings the ground is interpolated, so building sites are smoothed.
  • Not for engineering design, grading, flood elevations or surveying.
  • The edge of the coverage follows the 1 km lidar tiles, so it is a staircase.

Coordinates and heights. NAD83(2011) / UTM zone 12N (EPSG 6341), metres: the lidar's own coordinate system, with no datum transformation. This copy is for a local scene or a local ArcGIS Urban model in 6341. The other layers in the Millcreek LiDAR 2023 set are WGS84 / Web Mercator copies for global scenes; they hold the same data. Elevations are NAVD88 (Geoid18) in metres; heights are metres above the lidar bare-earth terrain.

Lidar source. The 2023 Salt Lake Valley QL1 lidar (USGS project UT_2023SaltLakeCo_C24, USGS 3D Elevation Program), flown on October 7, November 2 and November 4, 2023. Quality level 1 means at least 8 pulses per square metre and a vertical accuracy (RMSEz) of 10 cm or better on open, non-vegetated ground under the USGS specification; the vendor's own accuracy report for this project has not been reviewed for these products. For these products the building and vegetation classes of the points were refined; the ground class was not changed. All products are gridded at 0.5 m.

The Millcreek ArcGIS Urban layers. Three organization-only layers in UTM 12N for a local ArcGIS Urban model: 3D buildings (scene layer), building footprints (feature layer) and bare-earth terrain (elevation layer). They come from the same lidar, the same buildings and the same terrain as the public Millcreek LiDAR 2023 set.

Questions or corrections: City of Millcreek GIS. The processing records (scripts, checks and run logs) are kept by City of Millcreek GIS.
