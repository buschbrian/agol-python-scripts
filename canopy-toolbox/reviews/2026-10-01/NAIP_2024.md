# NAIP 2024 for tile 12TVL2804 (October 1, 2026)

Why: the 2021 NAIP we have used (flown **2021-11-13**) is two years from the lidar (2023-10-07 to 2023-11-05). The only aerial
collection found closer is **NAIP 2024, flown 2024-07-07 over this tile**, about eight months after the lidar. No collection in the
lidar's own window exists in UGRC's NAIP, Hexagon or HRO lists. Hexagon (15 cm, RGB only, licensed) is the same 2024 flight.

The tile straddles two quarter-quad sheets (`nw` starts at y 4504385, `sw` ends at 4504436), so both are needed. Each sheet is 0.6 m,
lossy JPEG (quality 65), labelled NAD83 UTM 12N (EPSG 26912), with RGB and B4 as separate files.

## The tile
`naip_local_tile.py` (tested in `tests/test_naip_local_tile.py`) cuts `naip_12TVL2804.tif` plus its metadata JSON: four 8-bit bands,
2000 x 2000 at 0.5 m, EPSG 6341, extent 428000 4504000 429000 4505000, no NoData. Same layout as the 2021 file from
`../2026-09-30/naip_fetch.py`, so `colorize_las.py --naip` and `naip_review.py` accept either. Run it again with `--flight-date`
for another tile; the date comes from UGRC's Hexagon service-dates layer, not from the sheets (they carry none).

## Checks
- Both sheets cover 204,000 cells (a 51 m strip). There they agree to a mean signed bias under 0.1 DN and a correlation of
  0.95 to 0.97 per band (mean absolute difference about 10 DN), so no radiometric seam; the first sheet in name order (`nw`) wins.
- **B4 is near-infrared by inference, not declaration**: 180 to 187 on visibly green pixels, 110 to 124 on pavement and roofs, 78 to 87 in
  shadow, correlation with luminance about 0.2. UGRC's files have no band description.
- NDVI from this tile: mean 0.072, 27.8% of pixels above 0.3, 2.7% above 0.5 (2021: 0.011, 15.2%). Greener, but low for July because RGB and
  B4 may not share a stretch, so it is **uncalibrated and not comparable with the 2021 NDVI**; use it as a relative cue.

## Limits
- Eight months and a summer after the lidar: crowns look fuller than the leaf-senescent points; new construction or removals since
  November 2023 show as mismatches that are not lidar error.
- The 26912 label is used as 6341 with no shift. UGRC states CE90 of 1.07 m and 2.6 m; register against the lidar (the SSD is
  needed) before trusting a sub-metre overlay.
- Hexagon licensed access is available to Millcreek but not used here.
